## Session 1: Lecture — Course Summary (30 min)

### What We Covered

| Weeks | Phase | Key Skills |
|-------|-------|------------|
| 1-2 | Clinical data foundations | Hospital information systems, ICD-10, ATC, LOINC coding |
| 3 | Data governance | PDPA, de-identification, IRB, ethical considerations |
| 4 | Study design | Cohort, case-control, cross-sectional designs from hospital data |
| 5 | Data quality | Missing data mechanisms, plausibility checks, temporal quality |
| 6 | Descriptive analysis | Table 1, summary statistics, lab reference ranges |
| 7 | Comparative analysis | Confounding, selection bias, information bias, adjustment strategies |
| 8 | Outcome measurement | Mortality, readmission, LOS, Charlson index, composite endpoints |
| 10-11 | Orange: Data preparation | Loading, cleaning, imputation, derived variables, filtering |
| 12 | Orange: Visualization | Box Plot, Distributions, Scatter Plot, Mosaic Display, Heat Map |
| 13 | Orange: Hypothesis testing | Group comparisons, logistic regression, stratified analysis |
| 14 | Orange: Outcome modeling | Train/test split, model training, ROC analysis, validation |

### The Clinical Data Analysis Workflow

1. **Define** a clinical question answerable with hospital data
2. **Design** the study — population, exposure, outcome, covariates (Week 4)
3. **Assess** data quality — missingness, plausibility, coding issues (Week 5)
4. **Describe** the study population — Table 1, distributions (Week 6)
5. **Compare** groups — identify and adjust for confounders (Week 7)
6. **Measure** outcomes — operationalize clinical endpoints (Week 8)
7. **Analyze** in Orange — clean, visualize, test, model (Weeks 10-14)
8. **Communicate** findings — structured report with clinical interpretation (Week 15)

> **Note:** This workflow is the same process used in published clinical research. The skills you practiced in this course directly apply to thesis work and clinical quality improvement projects.

## Session 2: Lecture — From Course to Thesis (20 min)

### How This Course Connects to Your Research

| Course Skill | Thesis Application |
|---|---|
| ICD-10/ATC/LOINC coding | Define study variables from hospital databases |
| PDPA and IRB process | Prepare ethics application for secondary data use |
| Study design | Write the Methods section of your thesis |
| Data quality assessment | Report data completeness and quality in your thesis |
| Table 1 and descriptive analysis | Characterize your study population |
| Confounding and bias | Justify your analytical choices in Discussion |
| Outcome measurement | Define primary and secondary endpoints |
| Orange workflow | Prototype your analysis before coding in R or Python |

### Recommended Next Steps

- **Data Mining** (if not taken) — hypothesis generation, causal reasoning, regression
- **Machine Learning** (if not taken) — prediction models, calibration, SHAP, fairness
- **Special Topic Research / Thesis** — apply these skills to your own clinical dataset

## Session 3: Grade Consultation (60 min)

### Final Grade Components

| Component | Weight | Source |
|-----------|--------|--------|
| Study plan (midterm) | 25% | Week 9 submission |
| Capstone project | 45% | Week 15 report and presentation |
| Open-book exam | 30% | Week 17 exam score |

### Grade Scale

| Letter Grade | Midpoint | Final Score Range |
|---|---|---|
| A | 87 | 80-100 |
| B | 76 | 70-79 |
| C | 66 | 60-69 |
| D | 61 | 50-59 |
| E | 0 | 0-49 |

**Passing threshold (graduate program):** 70 (B)

### Consultation Process

- Individual meetings with the instructor
- Review each graded component: study plan, capstone, exam
- Discuss feedback and areas for improvement
- Address any questions about grading

> **Note:** If you plan to use hospital clinical data in your thesis, this is a good time to discuss your research question and how the skills from this course can support your work.
